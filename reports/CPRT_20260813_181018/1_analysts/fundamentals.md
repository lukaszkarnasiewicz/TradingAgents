## Executive Summary
Copart, Inc. (**CPRT**) is a global leader in online vehicle auction and remarketing services, catering to insurance companies, auto lenders, car dealers, and retail buyers. The company continues to exhibit industry-leading financial characteristics: high profit margins, strong free cash flow generation, an exceptional balance sheet, and a dominant physical infrastructure moat. 

Following a pullback from its 52-week high of **$50.11** to its current level near **$30.07** (Market Cap: **$27.84 Billion**), CPRT's trailing P/E multiple has compressed to **17.95x** (Forward P/E of **17.18x**). Management capitalized on this valuation disconnect by completing a significant **$1.414 Billion share buyback** in the most recent reported quarter (Q3 FY26 ending April 30, 2026), reducing the ordinary share count from 966.8 million to 925.8 million.

---

## 1. Company Profile & Strategic Moat
* **Core Business:** Copart processes and sells salvage and clean-title vehicles via its proprietary online auction platform (VB3 technology).
* **Competitive Moat:** 
  1. **Real Estate Network:** Owns vast vehicle storage facilities worldwide, reflected in **$3.80 Billion** in net property, plant, and equipment (PPE). Land ownership creates a significant barrier to entry due to local zoning rules and land acquisition costs.
  2. **Two-Sided Market Network Effects:** High concentration of vehicle volume attracts global buyers across 190+ countries, driving higher auction returns for insurance sellers.
  3. **High Automation & Operating Efficiency:** Minimal marginal cost to run additional vehicle auctions online, delivering superior margins.
