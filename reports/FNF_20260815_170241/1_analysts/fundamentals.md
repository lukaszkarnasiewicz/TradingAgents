## 1. Executive Summary & Company Profile

**Fidelity National Financial, Inc. (FNF)** is a leading provider of title insurance and settlement services to the real estate and mortgage industries, alongside annuity and life insurance operations through its majority-owned subsidiaries. As the largest title insurance company in the United States, FNF's business performance is tightly coupled with real estate transaction volumes, mortgage origination activity, interest rate environments, and investment income generation.
