# Truist Financial Corporation (TFC) Fundamental Analysis Report

**Date:** May 17, 2026  
**Ticker:** TFC  
**Sector:** Financial Services  
**Industry:** Banks - Regional

---

### 1. Executive Summary
Truist Financial Corporation (TFC) continues to demonstrate resilience and a return to stability following a transformative period in 2023 and 2024. After a significant goodwill impairment in 2023 and the strategic divestiture of its insurance business in 2024, TFC has refocused on its core banking operations. Current fundamentals suggest a value-oriented opportunity with a strong emphasis on shareholder returns through dividends and share repurchases. Trading at a Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio of approximately 0.98, the market is currently valuing TFC near its book value, offering a potential margin of safety for value investors.

### 2. Financial Performance Analysis
#### **Revenue and Net Interest Income (NII)**
*   **Trailing Twelve Months (TTM) Revenue:** $18.66 billion.
*   **Quarterly Trend:** Revenue has remained remarkably stable over the last five quarters, fluctuating between $4.9 billion and $5.25 billion per quarter.
*   **Net Interest Income:** NII for Q1 2026 stood at $3.60 billion. While there was a slight decline from Q4 2025 ($3.70 billion), the company has successfully managed its interest expense ($2.26 billion in Q1 2026) amidst a fluctuating interest rate environment.
*   **Profitability:** TFC maintains a high profit margin of 29.62% and an operating margin of 38.99%, indicating efficient management of non-interest expenses.

#### **Earnings Per Share (EPS)**
*   **Current EPS (TTM):** $4.04.
*   **Forward EPS:** $5.13 (Expected).
*   **Growth:** The Q1 2026 Diluted EPS of $1.09 represents a steady improvement compared to the $0.87 reported in Q1 2025, signaling an upward trajectory in core earnings power.

### 3. Balance Sheet and Solvency
*   **Total Assets:** $548.98 billion (as of March 31, 2026), showing slight growth from year-end 2025.
*   **Capital Position:** Total Stockholders' Equity is $64.21 billion. The Tangible Book Value per share is approximately $29.59, while the total Book Value per share is $47.74.
*   **Debt Profile:** Total debt has increased slightly to $69.06 billion in Q1 2026 to support liquidity and lending operations.
*   **Asset Quality:** The investment portfolio remains diversified with $46.44 billion in Held-to-Maturity (HTM) securities and $60.69 billion in Available-for-Sale (AFS) securities.

### 4. Cash Flow and Shareholder Returns
TFC remains a premier choice for income-focused traders due to its robust capital return program.
*   **Dividend Yield:** 4.43%, supported by a consistent quarterly common stock dividend of approximately $645 million - $680 million.
*   **Share Repurchases:** TFC has become increasingly aggressive in buybacks, repurchasing $1.13 billion in capital stock in Q1 2026 alone, up from $500 million in previous quarters. This reduces share count and boosts EPS.
*   **Free Cash Flow:** Despite the capital-intensive nature of banking, TFC generated $5.74 billion in FCF in 2025.

### 5. Key Insights for Traders
*   **Value Proposition:** TFC is trading at a Forward P/E of 9.16, which is significantly lower than its TTM P/E of 11.62. This suggests that the market has not yet fully priced in the expected earnings growth for the coming year.
*   **Shareholder Alignment:** The combination of a 4.4% dividend and an accelerated buyback program (over $1B/quarter) provides a strong floor for the stock price.
*   **Risk Mitigation:** A Beta of 0.906 indicates that TFC is less volatile than the broader market, making it a suitable defensive play in the financial sector.
*   **Institutional Stability:** Following the $6 billion impairment in 2023, the "cleaning of the balance sheet" appears complete, with no major unusual items reported in the most recent quarter.
