### Executive Summary & Company Profile

**Fair Isaac Corporation (NYSE: FICO)** is a pioneer and market leader in predictive analytics, decision management, and credit scoring software. The company operates through two main business segments:
1. **Scores**: The industry-standard FICO® Score used by major banks, credit card issuers, mortgage lenders, and auto lenders to assess consumer credit risk.
2. **Software (FICO Platform & Applications)**: Enterprise software solutions designed for fraud detection, customer engagement, decision management, and loan originations.

As of **August 15, 2026**, FICO's market capitalization stands at approximately **$23.45 Billion**, with its stock trading near **$1,032.92**. The stock has experienced a significant valuation reset from its 52-week high of **$1,998.01** (52-week low: **$870.01**), currently trading below its 50-day moving average ($1,179.79) and 200-day moving average ($1,347.45).

### Key Financial Documents & Historical Trend Analysis

#### 1. Income Statement Highlights & Growth Trajectory
Over the past five quarters, FICO has demonstrated accelerating top-line growth and expanding profitability, driven by pricing power in its Scores segment and scale efficiency in its Software platform.

* **Revenue Growth**: Quarterly revenue expanded to **$674.19M** in Q3 FY26 (ended June 30, 2026), up **25.7% YoY** compared to $536.42M in Q3 FY25, following a peak of $691.68M in Q2 FY26. TTM Revenue reached **$2.39 Billion**.
* **Gross Margins**: Exceptional gross profitability remains a hallmark of FICO's capital-light model. Gross profit reached **$587.17M** in Q3 FY26 (**87.09% gross margin**), with TTM gross profit reaching **$2.04 Billion** (85.1% margin).
* **Operating Efficiency**: Operating income reached **$362.63M** in Q3 FY26 (**53.79% operating margin**), compared to $262.52M in Q3 FY25.
* **Net Income & EPS Acceleration**: Quarterly net income grew to **$237.17M** (+30.5% YoY), while diluted EPS jumped **41.2% YoY** from **$7.40 to $10.45**. TTM net income stands at **$815.02 Million**, yielding TTM EPS of **$34.53** and Forward EPS estimates of **$53.04**.

#### 2. Balance Sheet Structure & Financial Leverage
* **Liquidity & Working Capital**: FICO ended Q3 FY26 with **$248.44M** in cash and cash equivalents. Working capital stood positive at **$136.37M**, recovering from negative working capital levels in late 2025.
* **Debt Load & Leverage Expansion**: Total debt increased to **$5.60 Billion** as of June 30, 2026 (Net Debt: **$5.33 Billion**), up from $2.80 Billion a year prior. FICO raised long-term debt to fund its aggressive share repurchase initiative.
* **Interest Coverage**: Quarterly interest expense rose to **$59.88M** in Q3 FY26 (up from $32.90M in Q3 FY25). Despite higher debt servicing costs, operating income of $362.63M provides strong **6.06x interest coverage**.
* **Negative Stockholders' Equity**: Stockholders' equity stands at **-$4.10 Billion** (Book Value per share: -$189.71). This negative equity is a common outcome of FICO’s deliberate strategy of repurchasing shares using debt and free cash flow rather than operational weakness.

#### 3. Cash Flow Generation & Aggressive Capital Allocation
* **Operating & Free Cash Flow**: Operating cash flow hit **$380.44M** in Q3 FY26, bringing TTM Free Cash Flow (FCF) to **$774.41 Million**. Capital expenditures remain minimal ($10.1M in Q3 FY26), emphasizing the high cash conversion capability of software and scoring licenses.
* **Capital Return via Share Repurchases**:
  * In Q3 FY26, FICO deployed **$2.27 Billion** towards stock repurchases, retiring millions of shares.
  * Total diluted average shares fell from **24.58M in Q3 FY25** to **22.70M in Q3 FY26** (a **7.6% YoY reduction** in share count).
  * This relentless reduction in share count serves as a major force multiplier for per-share earnings growth.
