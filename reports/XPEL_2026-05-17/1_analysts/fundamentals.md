# Fundamental Analysis Report: XPEL, Inc. (XPEL)
**Date:** 2026-05-17

## Executive Summary
XPEL, Inc. continues to demonstrate resilient growth within the automotive aftermarket sector, specializing in protective films and coatings. Over the last three fiscal years, the company has successfully scaled its revenue from $324 million in 2022 to over $476 million in 2025. With a current market capitalization of approximately $1.14 billion and a trailing P/E ratio of 21.7x, XPEL presents a growth-oriented profile with strong profitability and a robust balance sheet. The company's strategy involves a mix of organic expansion and aggressive inorganic growth through acquisitions, funded primarily by internal cash flow.

---

## 1. Financial Performance Analysis

### Revenue and Profitability
*   **Top-Line Growth:** XPEL has maintained a consistent upward trajectory. Revenue grew by ~20% in 2025 compared to 2024. The trailing twelve-month (TTM) revenue stands at $489.7 million.
*   **Margin Expansion:** Gross margins have improved from ~39% in 2022 to ~42% in 2025. This indicates strong pricing power and effective cost management despite inflationary pressures.
*   **Earnings:** Net income for 2025 reached $51.2 million. While there was a slight dip in 2024 ($45.5M) due to a spike in operating expenses (SG&A increased from $95M to $118M), the company rebounded in 2025, showing better scalability of its operational model.

### Quarterly Trends
The most recent quarterly data (Q4 2025) shows revenue of $122.3 million with a net income of $13.4 million. The forward EPS is projected at $2.42, suggesting that analysts expect earnings to grow significantly from the current TTM EPS of $1.91.

---

## 2. Balance Sheet and Liquidity
XPEL maintains an exceptionally healthy balance sheet, which provides a significant "moat" during economic volatility.

*   **Liquidity:** The Current Ratio is a strong **3.07**, with $230.7 million in current assets against only $71 million in current liabilities.
*   **Cash Position:** As of the end of 2025, XPEL held **$50.9 million** in cash and cash equivalents, a substantial increase from the $22.1 million held at the end of 2024.
*   **Debt Profile:** Total debt is modest at **$22.9 million**, largely composed of capital lease obligations. The company is effectively "net cash" positive if excluding leases, representing very low financial risk.
*   **Inventory Management:** Inventory levels are high at $122.8 million. While this ensures product availability, it also represents a potential risk if demand for high-end automotive films softens.

---

## 3. Cash Flow and Capital Allocation
XPEL’s cash flow generation is one of its strongest fundamental attributes.

*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** FCF jumped to **$61.4 million** in 2025 from $39.2 million in 2024. This provides the company with ample dry powder for its "buy-and-build" strategy.
*   **Acquisitions:** The company is an active acquirer. In 2025 alone, it spent **$26.2 million** on business purchases. These acquisitions are typically focused on local distributors and installers to capture more of the value chain.
*   **Share Repurchases:** In 2025, the company initiated a modest share buyback of ~$3 million, signaling management's confidence in the stock's value.

---

## 4. Strategic Insights for Traders

*   **Valuation:** With a Forward P/E of **17.1x**, XPEL is trading at a discount compared to its historical growth rates and its TTM P/E of 21.7x. For a company growing double-digits with 20%+ ROE, this may represent an attractive entry point.
*   **Efficiency:** A Return on Equity (ROE) of **20.27%** highlights management's ability to generate high returns on shareholder capital.
*   **Operating Leverage:** Traders should monitor SG&A expenses. If XPEL can keep operating expense growth below revenue growth, the "operating leverage" will drive significant EPS surprises to the upside.
*   **Risk Factors:** The primary risks include a slowdown in the luxury automotive market (XPEL's primary end-market) and the integration risk of its frequent acquisitions.
