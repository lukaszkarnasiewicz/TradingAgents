# Fundamental Research Report: MercadoLibre, Inc. (MELI)
**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Ticker:** MELI

## 1. Executive Summary
MercadoLibre (MELI) continues to demonstrate strong top-line growth as the dominant e-commerce and fintech player in Latin America. While the company is scaling efficiently in terms of revenue, its profitability margins remain modest, and financial leverage (debt-to-equity) has increased notably. Given the high growth prospects but also the significant debt load and premium valuation, a **HOLD** recommendation is warranted at this time for conservative traders, while growth-oriented traders may see continued potential.

## 2. Company Overview
MercadoLibre, Inc. operates an online marketplace and fintech platform (Mercado Pago) in Latin America. It sits at the intersection of consumer cyclical and technology-driven financial services, making it a pivotal stock for tracking Latin American economic growth and digital adoption.

## 3. Financial Analysis

### Income Statement Trends
*   **Revenue:** Sustained strong growth trajectory, reaching over $28.89 billion in 2025 (up from $20.78 billion in 2024).
*   **Profitability:** Net income has grown consistently, reaching $1.997 billion in 2025. However, net profit margins are constrained at approximately 6.9% (operating margin), indicating intense competition and heavy investment in logistics and fintech infrastructure.

### Balance Sheet & Financial Health
*   **Leverage:** Total debt has escalated to $11.39 billion in 2025 from $6.85 billion in 2024. The Debt-to-Equity ratio is high (169.98%), which warrants close monitoring, especially in volatile interest rate environments.
*   **Liquidity:** The current ratio stands at 1.16, indicating moderate short-term liquidity. The company has substantial restricted cash ($10.65 billion), which is likely related to its fintech/payment operations.

### Cash Flow
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Demonstrates strong and improving capability to generate cash from operations ($12.1 billion in 2025).
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Despite high operating cash flow, heavy investment (CAPEX) in logistics and infrastructure leads to a more nuanced FCF picture when considering all non-cash items, though the core cash-generating capability is impressive.

## 5. Actionable Insights
*   **Monitor Margin Expansion:** Watch for improvements in operating margins. If MELI cannot improve efficiency as it scales, its valuation may become hard to justify if growth slows.
*   **Debt Management:** Traders should pay close attention to quarterly reports regarding debt service costs. Elevated debt levels in the current inflationary/interest rate environment increase the risk profile.
*   **Fintech Adoption:** Mercado Pago's expansion is crucial. Its performance often serves as a proxy for the overall health of the digital economy in LatAm.
*   **Technical Context:** The current stock price (~$1,738) is well below the 200-day average (~$2,058), suggesting downward pressure. Traders should look for stability around the 50-day average or a clear technical reversal signal.
