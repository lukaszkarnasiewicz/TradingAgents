## 1. Company Profile & Overview
Arch Capital Group Ltd. (ACGL) is a prominent player in the global property, casualty, and mortgage insurance and reinsurance markets. Operating through its subsidiaries, ACGL underwrites a diverse book of specialty insurance and reinsurance risks worldwide, benefiting from disciplined underwriting standards, strong capital adequacy, and strategic capital management.
