# Chubb Limited (CB) Fundamental Analysis Report

**Date:** May 17, 2026  
**Ticker:** CB

**Industry:** Insurance - Property & Casualty

### **1. Executive Summary**
Chubb Limited (CB) continues to demonstrate exceptional financial strength and operational efficiency in the Property & Casualty insurance sector. Over the last four fiscal years, the company has successfully scaled its revenue while more than doubling its net income. With a disciplined approach to underwriting and a robust investment portfolio, Chubb is positioned as a premier defensive and growth-at-a-reasonable-price (GARP) candidate for investors.

### **2. Financial Performance Analysis**
#### **Revenue and Earnings Growth**
Chubb has exhibited a clear upward trajectory in its top and bottom lines:
*   **Revenue Growth:** Total revenue grew from **$43.06 billion in 2022** to **$59.78 billion in 2025**, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 11.5%.
*   **Net Income Expansion:** Most impressively, net income attributable to common stockholders surged from **$5.25 billion in 2022** to **$10.31 billion in 2025**. 
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** Diluted EPS has effectively doubled, rising from **$12.55 in 2022** to **$25.68 in 2025**. The current TTM EPS stands at **$28.27**, reflecting continued momentum in the first half of 2026.

#### **Profitability Margins**
*   **Profit Margin:** 18.53%
*   **Operating Margin:** 20.64%
*   **Return on Equity (ROE):** 15.43%
Chubb’s ROE is strong for the insurance industry, indicating efficient use of shareholder capital.

### **3. Financial Position & Balance Sheet Strength**
#### **Asset Quality and Capitalization**
*   **Total Assets:** Grew to **$272.33 billion** by end-of-year 2025, supported by a massive investment portfolio of **$157.97 billion**.
*   **Book Value:** The current book value per share is **$189.93**, with a Price-to-Book (P/B) ratio of **1.71**, suggesting the stock is trading at a fair premium given its high return on equity.
*   **Debt Profile:** Chubb maintains a conservative capital structure. The **Debt-to-Equity ratio of 31.46%** is manageable and typical for a large-cap insurer, providing significant liquidity and solvency headroom.

### **4. Cash Flow and Shareholder Returns**
Chubb is a cash-generating powerhouse, allowing for substantial capital return to shareholders:
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Generated **$12.62 billion (TTM)**. In 2025, operating cash flow was **$12.82 billion**.
*   **Share Buybacks:** The company has aggressively reduced its share count. Common stock repurchases reached **$3.69 billion in 2025**, up from $1.8 billion in 2024. Shares outstanding dropped from 414.6 million in 2022 to approximately 391.1 million by end-of-year 2025.
*   **Dividends:** Maintains a consistent dividend payout (currently **$1.51 billion annually**), yielding approximately **1.2%**.

### **5. Actionable Insights for Traders**
*   **Value Strategy:** With a **Forward PE of 11.09x**, Chubb is trading at a significant discount to the broader market (S&P 500) despite its superior earnings growth. Traders should view this as a potential "Value" play with a "Growth" kicker.
*   **Defensive Positioning:** A **Beta of 0.44** indicates the stock is less than half as volatile as the market. This makes CB an excellent "flight-to-safety" instrument during periods of market turbulence.
*   **Technical Support:** The stock is trading above its 200-day moving average ($302.68), currently hovering near its 50-day average ($326.00), suggesting a healthy long-term uptrend.
