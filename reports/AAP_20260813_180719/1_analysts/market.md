### 1. Selection & Suitability of Technical Indicators

To evaluate `AAP`'s current market setup, **8 non-redundant, complementary technical indicators** were selected across moving averages, momentum, volatility, and volume analysis:

1. **10-Day Exponential Moving Average (`close_10_ema`)**: Captures responsive short-term price momentum to detect immediate trend changes and short-term entry/exit triggers.
2. **50-Day Simple Moving Average (`close_50_sma`)**: Acts as a medium-term trend benchmark and dynamic support/resistance filter.
3. **200-Day Simple Moving Average (`close_200_sma`)**: Identifies structural, long-term trend direction and key institutional support/resistance levels.
4. **Moving Average Convergence Divergence (`macd`)**: Measures shifts in trend directional momentum and highlights potential trend reversals or continuations.
5. **Relative Strength Index (`rsi`)**: Evaluates underlying buying vs. selling pressure to identify overbought (>70) or oversold (<30) conditions.
6. **Bollinger Upper Band (`boll_ub`)**: Measures top-end volatility expansion and potential dynamic resistance zones (~2 standard deviations above 20 SMA).
7. **Bollinger Lower Band (`boll_lb`)**: Measures bottom-end volatility expansion and dynamic oversold rebound targets (~2 standard deviations below 20 SMA).
8. **Volume-Weighted Moving Average (`vwma`)**: Combines volume and price action to reflect institutional positioning and volume-supported baseline levels.
