While an RSI near 30 often suggests a potential bounce, in a strong downtrend, RSI can remain "embedded" in oversold territory for an extended period. The lack of a sharp reversal at 30 indicates that selling pressure remains dominant.

The price is currently riding the **Lower Bollinger Band (23.31)**, which confirms high volatility and extreme bearishness. This suggests that investors who bought recently are "underwater," which creates significant overhead supply (selling pressure) on any attempt at a rally.
