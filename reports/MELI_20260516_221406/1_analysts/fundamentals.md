# MercadoLibre, Inc. (MELI) Fundamental Analysis Report

**Date:** 2026-05-16  
**Ticker:** MELI  
**Sector:** Consumer Cyclical  
**Industry:** Internet Retail

---

### 1. Company Profile & Business Overview
MercadoLibre, Inc. (MELI) is the leading e-commerce and fintech ecosystem in Latin America. Operating across multiple countries, most notably Brazil, Mexico, and Argentina, the company provides a comprehensive suite of services including its marketplace (Mercado Libre), payments and fintech solutions (Mercado Pago), logistics (Mercado Envios), advertising (Mercado Ads), and credit (Mercado Credito).

### 2. Financial Performance Analysis

#### Revenue and Profitability
MELI has demonstrated exceptional revenue growth over the last several years.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Annual revenue soared from **$10.78B in 2022** to **$28.89B in 2025**, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 39%. Trailing Twelve Month (TTM) revenue as of May 2026 stands at **$31.80B**.
*   **EBITDA Expansion:** EBITDA has scaled significantly from **$1.32B in 2022** to **$3.82B in 2025**, showing the benefits of operational leverage as the platform matures.
*   **Net Income:** The company has successfully pivoted from a high-growth, low-margin phase to a highly profitable one. Net income grew from **$482M in 2022** to **$1.99B in 2025**.

#### Margins
*   **Gross Margin:** Remains robust at approximately **49%** (TTM Gross Profit of $15.73B on $31.8B revenue).
*   **Net Profit Margin:** Currently stands at **6.04%**, reflecting the competitive nature of the retail sector and heavy reinvestment into technology and logistics.

### 3. Balance Sheet & Solvency
*   **Liquidity:** MELI maintains a healthy liquidity position with a **Current Ratio of 1.16**. Total Assets have ballooned to **$42.67B**, driven largely by increases in loans receivable ($8.85B) and restricted cash ($10.65B).
*   **Debt Profile:** The company has a **Debt-to-Equity ratio of 169.9%**, which appears high. Total debt reached **$11.39B** by the end of 2025. However, this is offset by a massive cash and short-term investment position of approximately **$13.54B** (End Cash Position), suggesting the debt is manageable and likely used to fund the expansion of the Mercado Credito portfolio.
*   **Equity Growth:** Stockholders' equity has grown from **$1.83B in 2022** to **$6.75B in 2025**, indicating strong internal capital generation.

### 4. Cash Flow & Liquidity
*   **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** MELI is a cash-generating powerhouse. OCF was **$12.12B in 2025**, up from $2.94B in 2022.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** Despite heavy capital expenditures ($1.34B in 2025), the company generated **$10.77B in FCF** for the full year 2025. This provides significant "dry powder" for further acquisitions or organic expansion.

### 5. Valuation & Market Sentiment
*   **PE Ratio (TTM):** At **40.81**, the valuation is high compared to the broader market but typical for high-growth tech companies.
*   **Forward PE:** Significantly lower at **25.70**, suggesting analysts expect strong earnings growth to continue.
*   **PEG Ratio:** A PEG of **0.99** indicates that the stock is potentially fairly valued relative to its expected growth rate (a PEG around 1.0 is often considered "fair value").
*   **Beta:** At **1.41**, the stock is more volatile than the overall market, which is expected for a high-growth consumer/tech play in emerging markets.

---

### 6. Actionable Insights for Traders

*   **Bull Case:** MELI's ecosystem (Fintech + E-commerce + Logistics) creates a "flywheel" effect that is difficult for competitors to break. The low PEG ratio (0.99) suggests that despite the high nominal price, the growth justifies the entry. The massive FCF generation provides a safety net and capital for aggressive expansion.
*   **Bear Case:** High exposure to Latin American macroeconomic volatility and currency fluctuations (evidenced by the -$739M impact of exchange rate changes in 2024). The high Debt-to-Equity ratio might be a concern if credit markets tighten or if the Mercado Credito default rates rise unexpectedly.
*   **Trading Strategy:** MELI is currently trading below its 200-day average ($2,057) but near its 50-day average ($1,738). Traders might look for a "value" entry point if it stabilizes above the 50-day SMA, targeting a return toward the 52-week high of $2,645 as earnings continue to compound.
