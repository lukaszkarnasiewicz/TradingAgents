### **Technical Analysis Report: MELI**

#### **1. Trend Analysis: Moving Averages**
MELI is currently in a confirmed bearish trend across multiple timeframes. 
- **Long-Term Trend**: The price ($1,546.81) is trading significantly below its **200-day SMA ($2,057.88)**. This indicates a "Death Cross" environment where the long-term sentiment is overwhelmingly negative.

- **Analysis**: The wide gap between the current price and the 200 SMA (nearly 25% difference) suggests the stock is "extended" to the downside. In historical contexts, such extreme deviations often lead to a mean-reversion rally toward the moving averages, though they do not guarantee a trend reversal.

#### **3. Volatility and Price Range: Bollinger Bands**
- **Bollinger Bands**: The price of $1,546.81 is currently riding the **Lower Bollinger Band ($1,512.84)**. 
- **Observation**: When a stock price "walks the band" to the downside, it indicates a high-volatility sell-off. The fact that the price is stabilizing near the lower band suggests a period of consolidation or a "relief bounce" may be approaching, as the move has reached a statistical extreme (2 standard deviations).
