### Indicator Selection Rationale

To analyze **Zoetis Inc. (`ZTS`)** effectively, **8 complementary indicators** were selected across Moving Averages, Momentum, Volatility, and Volume categories to provide a holistic view of trend direction, momentum changes, structural support/resistance, and market volatility without introducing redundant signals:

1. **`close_10_ema` (Short-Term EMA)**: Tracks short-term price momentum and immediate directional shifts.
2. **`close_50_sma` (Medium-Term SMA)**: Establishes medium-term trend context and dynamic resistance/support level.
3. **`close_200_sma` (Long-Term SMA)**: Provides long-term macro trend baseline and highlights structural regime shifts.
4. **`vwma` (Volume-Weighted Moving Average)**: Integrates volume with price action to validate the strength of price levels and trend sustainability.
5. **`macd` (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)**: Captures momentum acceleration, trend shifts, and signal crossovers.
6. **`rsi` (Relative Strength Index)**: Measures speed and magnitude of recent price changes to identify overbought/oversold momentum conditions without indicator duplication.
7. **`boll_ub` & `boll_lb` (Bollinger Bands - Upper & Lower)**: Defines volatility boundaries and range extreme limits to detect support/resistance testing.
8. **`atr` (Average True Range)**: Quantifies absolute price volatility to establish precise stop-loss levels and risk management parameters.
