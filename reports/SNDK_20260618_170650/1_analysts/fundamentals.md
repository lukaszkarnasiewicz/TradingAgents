### **Company Overview: Sandisk Corporation (SNDK)**
Sandisk Corporation is a major player in the **Technology** sector, specifically within the **Computer Hardware** industry. Historically known for flash memory products, the provided data depicts a company that has evolved into a high-growth powerhouse with a massive market capitalization of **$322.21 billion**. The company has demonstrated an extraordinary turnaround and expansion over the last fiscal year, transitioning from net losses to multi-billion dollar quarterly profits.

---

### **Financial Performance Analysis**

#### **1. Revenue and Profitability**
SNDK is currently experiencing a period of hyper-growth. 
*   **Revenue Growth:** Quarterly revenue surged from **$1.69 billion** in March 2025 to **$5.95 billion** in March 2026—a 250% increase year-over-year. The jump from Q4 2025 ($3.03B) to Q1 2026 ($5.95B) alone represents nearly 100% sequential growth.
*   **Margin Expansion:** The company’s efficiency has improved dramatically. 
    *   **Gross Margin:** Increased from ~22.5% in early 2025 to a staggering **78.3%** in Q1 2026.
    *   **Operating Margin:** Currently sits at an industry-leading **69.9%**.
*   **Net Income:** After reporting a loss of $1.93 billion in March 2025 (largely due to a massive $1.83B impairment charge), SNDK has consistently grown its bottom line, reaching **$3.62 billion** in net income for the quarter ending March 2026.

#### **2. Earnings Per Share (EPS)**
The earnings trajectory is highly bullish.
*   **TTM EPS:** $29.28.
*   **Quarterly EPS (Diluted):** Rose from a loss of ($13.33) in March 2025 to a gain of **$23.03** in March 2026.
*   **Forward EPS:** Estimated at **$183.05**, suggesting that analysts expect the current momentum to translate into significantly higher annual earnings, potentially driven by further scaling or high-margin product launches.

### **Balance Sheet and Liquidity**

SNDK’s balance sheet has strengthened remarkably, characterized by aggressive deleveraging and cash accumulation.

*   **Cash Position:** Cash and equivalents grew from $1.51 billion to **$3.74 billion** in one year.
*   **Debt Reduction:** Total debt plummeted from $2.14 billion in March 2025 to just **$182 million** in March 2026. This aggressive repayment of debt significantly lowers the company's financial risk and interest burden.
*   **Liquidity:** A **Current Ratio of 4.78** indicates exceptional short-term liquidity, allowing the company to easily fund operations and future R&D.
*   **Receivables Risk:** Accounts receivable jumped from $1.24 billion (Dec 2025) to **$2.73 billion** (Mar 2026). While this aligns with the revenue growth, traders should monitor the collection period to ensure credit quality remains high.

### **Cash Flow Highlights**
*   **Operating Cash Flow:** Reached **$3.04 billion** in the most recent quarter, up from a mere $26 million a year prior.
*   **Free Cash Flow (FCF):** SNDK generated **$2.99 billion** in FCF in the last quarter. This high FCF-to-Net-Income conversion ratio highlights the high quality of its earnings.
*   **Capital Expenditures:** CapEx remains relatively low (~$45M per quarter), suggesting that the company's growth is coming from highly scalable software or existing hardware capacity rather than capital-intensive factory expansions.

*   **Deleveraging Success:** By nearly wiping out its debt, SNDK has cleared its path for potential dividends, buybacks, or strategic acquisitions.
