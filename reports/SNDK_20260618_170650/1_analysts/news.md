### **SNDK: A Structural Leader in the AI Memory Cycle**

**SanDisk (SNDK)** is currently at the center of a "perfect storm" of high demand and restricted supply. The stock has experienced a historic rally, gaining over 4,400% in the last 52 weeks, and is currently trading near its 52-week high at **$2,144.40**.

**Key Drivers for SNDK:**
1.  **Pure-Play Transition (June 22 Catalyst):** A pivotal corporate event is scheduled for June 22, 2026. Western Digital (WDC) will complete a share swap that establishes **SNDK** as a pure-play NAND and SSD company. This separation is expected to unlock significant value by allowing investors to target the memory cycle directly.
2.  **Apple Pricing Confirmation:** A major catalyst this week was Apple's explicit confirmation that it is passing through increased memory costs to consumers. This validates the "structural shortage" thesis, signaling that NAND prices have substantial room to rise without destroying demand.
3.  **AI Infrastructure Demand:** The demand for high-performance storage in AI data centers is unprecedented. Analysts have raised **SNDK**'s earnings estimates through 2028, citing the rapid expansion of AI-specific hardware (TPUs and ASICs) that require massive NAND arrays.
4.  **Extreme Scarcity:** The shortage is so severe that consumer-grade products, such as SanDisk’s PlayStation 5 SSDs, are retailing for nearly $3,000, illustrating the pricing power currently held by memory manufacturers.

*   **Valuation Caution:** While the structural story is strong, **SNDK** is technically "the most overbought stock in history." Traders should use trailing stops to protect gains against a potential "sell the news" reaction after the June 22 event.
