# Fundamental Analysis Report: Brookfield Corporation (BN)
**Date:** May 17, 2026
**Ticker:** BN (Brookfield Corporation)
**Sector:** Financial Services
**Industry:** Asset Management

## Executive Summary
Brookfield Corporation (BN) is a leading global alternative asset manager focusing on real estate, renewable power, infrastructure, and private equity. As of May 2026, the company presents a complex fundamental profile characterized by massive asset scale ($519B), high leverage, and a significant projected turnaround in earnings. While current trailing earnings are depressed, leading to a high TTM PE, forward-looking metrics suggest a much more attractive valuation if the company executes its growth and deleveraging strategies.

---

## 1. Financial Performance & Profitability
Brookfield’s top-line revenue has seen some contraction over the last three years, falling from a peak of $95.9B in 2023 to $75.1B in 2025. However, TTM revenue has shown signs of stabilization at $79.1B.

*   **Earnings Quality:** Net Income has been volatile, hitting $1.3B in 2025, down from $2.1B in 2022. This volatility is largely driven by "Other Income/Expense" items, including asset revaluations and gains/losses on sales, which are inherent to the asset management business model.
*   **Operating Efficiency:** Despite declining revenue, Operating Income remains robust at approximately $18B. The Operating Margin stands at a healthy 28.92%, though the final Profit Margin is thin (1.69%) due to high interest costs.
*   **The "Forward" Story:** There is a stark contrast between the **TTM PE (89.29)** and the **Forward PE (7.76)**. Analysts are forecasting a massive jump in EPS from $0.51 to $5.87. This suggests either a major cyclical recovery in their underlying assets or a significant reduction in interest-bearing debt/expenses.

## 2. Balance Sheet & Leverage Analysis
Brookfield operates with a capital-intensive model that relies heavily on leverage.
*   **Debt Profile:** Total Debt has climbed to **$259.6B** as of late 2025. The **Debt to Equity ratio of 161.47%** is high, and the interest expense ($17.1B) consumed nearly 95% of operating income in 2025. This makes BN highly sensitive to the interest rate environment.
*   **Liquidity:** The **Current Ratio of 0.89** indicates tight short-term liquidity, though the company maintains a substantial cash pile of $16.2B and has a proven ability to access capital markets.
*   **Asset Base:** Total assets reached $519B. Notably, "Investment Properties" decreased from $124B to $85.6B between 2023 and 2025, suggesting a strategic shift or successful divestment of real estate assets during that period.

## 3. Cash Flow & Capital Allocation
*   **Operating Cash Flow (OCF):** A bright spot is the steady growth in OCF, which rose from $6.5B in 2023 to **$10.96B in 2025**. This indicates that the core operations are generating increasing amounts of cash despite the reported net income volatility.
*   **Capital Expenditures:** CapEx has increased significantly to **$14.8B**, leading to negative Free Cash Flow (-$3.8B). This suggests Brookfield is in a heavy "reinvestment" phase, pouring capital into new infrastructure or renewable projects.
*   **Shareholder Returns:** The company remains committed to share buybacks, spending **$1.19B** on repurchases in 2025. The dividend yield remains modest at 0.61%, signaling that management prefers reinvestment and buybacks over high dividend payouts.

## 4. Actionable Insights for Traders
*   **Interest Rate Sensitivity:** BN is a "proxy" for interest rate trends. Any signaling of rate cuts by central banks could significantly lower its $17B annual interest burden, directly boosting the bottom line and potentially justifying the low Forward PE.
*   **Earnings Rebound Play:** The massive discrepancy between TTM and Forward EPS suggests a "coiled spring" effect. Traders should look for quarterly earnings beats as confirmation that the projected $5.87 EPS is achievable.
*   **Valuation vs. Book Value:** With a **Price to Book (P/B) of 3.63**, the stock is trading well above its accounting book value ($12.56). This implies the market is pricing in significant "intangible" value in their asset management franchise and future carried interest.
*   **Technical Context:** The stock is trading at $42.91, below its 200-day average ($44.88) but above its 52-week low ($37.54). A break above the 200-day average could signal a shift in sentiment toward the "forward growth" narrative.
